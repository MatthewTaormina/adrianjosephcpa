Critical Questions Every Business Founder Must Address

1. Operational Leadership

Who will manage the business in your absence? We help families distinguish between management capability and equity ownership, establishing governance systems that protect operating performance.

2. Tax-Free LCGE Purification

To qualify for the Lifetime Capital Gains Exemption (saving over $250,000 in personal taxes per shareholder), 90%+ of corporate assets must be used in active business. We purify balance sheets well ahead of a sale.
