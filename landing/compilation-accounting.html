Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Compilation Engagement (CSRS 4200)?

A Compilation Engagement (formerly called a Notice to Reader) is an official engagement where a licensed CPA assists management in compiling financial information into formal financial statements. It includes a compilation communication letter and specific basis of accounting notes required by major Canadian chartered banks.

Can you help if our QuickBooks file is messy or months behind?

Yes! As a certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor, Tania Joseph specializes in catch-up reconciliations and cleanups. We reconcile bank and credit card accounts, correct sales tax (GST/HST) filing mismatches, and establish automated bank rules so your bookkeeping stays accurate going forward.
