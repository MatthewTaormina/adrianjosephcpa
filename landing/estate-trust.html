Answers to Your Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if an executor distributes estate funds without a CRA Clearance Certificate?

Under Section 159(2) of the Canadian Income Tax Act, an executor who distributes estate assets without first receiving a formal Clearance Certificate from the CRA remains personally liable for any unpaid taxes owed by the deceased up to the value of the distributed assets. We prepare the TX19 clearance application to protect executors completely.

When should an entrepreneur consider an estate freeze?

An estate freeze is most effective when your operating business has substantial equity value and strong future growth prospects. Implementing the freeze early locks in your tax liability and transfers all subsequent multi-million dollar growth to your successors tax-free until a sale.

How often should our family review our estate plan?

We recommend reviewing your estate structure every 3 to 5 years, or whenever major life milestones occur (marriages, children, business expansion, or federal legislative changes such as trust reporting and capital gains inclusion changes).
