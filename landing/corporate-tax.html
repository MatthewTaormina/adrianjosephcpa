Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Section 85 rollover save my business taxes?

A Section 85 election under the Canadian Income Tax Act allows you to transfer appreciated assets (such as an operating business, real estate, or shares) into a corporation in exchange for shares without triggering immediate capital gains tax. You legally defer taxes until the new shares are sold.
