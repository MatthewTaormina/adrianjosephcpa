Client Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

What information should I prepare for an initial corporate tax review?

For incorporated businesses, having copies of your most recent Notice of Assessment (NOA), prior year's T2 corporate tax return, and current trial balance or QuickBooks balance sheet helps us quickly diagnose opportunities for tax deferral or reorganization.

Can you collaborate directly with our family lawyer on estate planning?

Yes. Effective estate and trust planning requires seamless coordination between legal counsel (who drafts wills, trust deeds, and powers of attorney) and a specialized tax accountant. As a TEP practitioner, Adrian Joseph regularly collaborates with clients' legal counsel or provides referrals to experienced estate solicitors.

Do you work with clients outside of Pickering and Durham Region?

Absolutely. While our physical corporate office is located in Pickering, Ontario, we serve clients throughout Toronto, York Region, Peel Region, and across Canada via secure cloud portals and video conferencing.
