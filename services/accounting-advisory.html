Family Wealth Continuity

Family Business Succession Planning

Sooner or later, every business founder wants to retire. Business succession planning should be a top priority for every family enterprise. Besides ensuring that you can afford to retire comfortably, what happens to the business is paramount to your ongoing financial security.

Critical Questions Every Founder Must Answer:

  • Who is going to manage your business operations in your absence?
  • How will equity and voting ownership be transferred among active vs non-active children?
  • Will your business carry on within the family or will it be prepared for a third-party acquisition?

We are adept at helping you assess all available tax-deferred transition options, establishing an estate freeze, and crafting an executable roadmap for the successful transition of your business.

The Succession Roadmap Process

Step 1: Valuation & Tax Position Audit

Determine fair market value and verify Qualified Small Business Corporation share status.

Step 2: Estate Freeze Implementation

Lock in current founder value into fixed preferred shares, passing future growth to successors.

Step 3: Governance & Operational Handover

Transition key customer relationships, banking covenants, and signing authority smoothly.
