Specialized Practice Modules

Comprehensive Tax Planning Capabilities

Corporate Reorganizations & Restructuring

Adjusting corporate structures based on growing operational requirements, asset protection, and tax efficiency.

  • Section 85 tax-deferred asset rollovers into new holding entities.
  • Bringing new shareholders or key management into the corporation tax-efficiently.
  • Transitioning ownership to the next generation without triggering immediate capital gains.
  • Converting unincorporated sole proprietorships and partnerships into limited corporations.
  • Eliminating inactive corporations through amalgamations and clean wind-ups.

Purchase or Sale of a Business

Maximizing after-tax proceeds on sales and conducting thorough financial diligence during acquisitions.

  • Structuring transactions for share purchases vs. asset purchases.
  • Securing the Lifetime Capital Gains Exemption (LCGE) for Qualified Small Business Corporation (QSBC) shares.
  • Comprehensive financial due diligence on target entity tax compliance and liabilities.
  • Completing all required CRA election forms and transaction tax filings.

Separation & Divorce Tax Optimization

Tax rules surrounding matrimonial breakdown and property division are intricate and fraught with hidden penalties.

  • Reviewing tax aspects based on taxpayer financial assets and corporate shares.
  • Collaborating closely with your family lawyer to structure property settlements optimally.
  • Tax-free rollovers under Section 73(1) and spousal support tax deductions.

General Family Tax Planning & Wealth Deferral

Structuring family wealth to legally reduce marginal tax rates across generations.

  • Establishing prescribed interest rate loans to split income with spouses or minor children.
  • Using family trusts, holding companies, and corporate-owned life insurance.
  • Specific corporate compensation structuring for owner-managers and key family employees.

Deceased Taxpayers & Estate Administration

Mitigating the deemed disposition of capital property upon death and managing terminal tax liabilities.

  • Reviewing tax positions and elective returns to access multiple basic personal tax credits.
  • Working directly with executors to file final returns and post-mortem estate returns.
  • Obtaining Section 159(2) CRA Clearance Certificates to protect executors from personal liability.

Immigration & Emigration (Cross-Border Tax)

Navigating Canadian tax residency rules when entering or permanently departing the country.

  • Tax planning for new immigrants to establish optimal Canadian cost-basis on worldwide assets.
  • Emigration tax planning to minimize departure tax and manage non-resident withholding taxes.
  • Foreign reporting (T1135, T1134) compliance for offshore assets and investments.

Capital Gains Planning & Real Estate

Capital gains legislation requires active planning, particularly for business owners and property investors across Ontario:

Principal Residence Exemption Planning

Evaluating designation options for clients owning multiple personal-use properties (such as homes and cottages) to optimize the exemption over holding periods.

QSBC Share Purification

Advising on structuring and "purifying" operating corporations so that 90%+ of assets are active business assets, preserving share eligibility for the multi-hundred thousand dollar capital gains exemption.
