Cross-Border Tax Planning: Immigration & Emigration

Entering or leaving Canada triggers deemed disposition rules, departure taxes, and complex foreign property reporting (T1135).

  • Deemed acquisition cost step-up upon establishing Canadian residency.
  • Departure tax minimization when becoming non-resident for tax purposes.
  • Foreign affiliate, trust, and property reporting compliance.
