Executive Profile

A Trusted Advisor to Canadian Entrepreneurs & Private Families

Having over 25 years of experience working alongside Canadian entrepreneurs, Adrian Joseph is highly referred by clients who value his warm demeanor and uncompromising depth of expertise in small & medium-size business tax and financial structuring.

Adrian started his professional career in the management consulting division of a Big Four accounting firm. He later transitioned into the Canadian craft beer sector, playing a pivotal role in the expansion and financial stewardship of Steam Whistle Brewing ↗, overseeing its corporate finance function for many years before establishing his independent practice.

Early in his career, Adrian recognized the critical synergy between finance and commercial strategy, completing the Chartered Institute of Marketing (UK) examinations (ACIM). This rare dual qualification in marketing and accounting enables him to look beyond dry spreadsheets and understand the true entrepreneurial drivers of enterprise value.

In developing his specialized taxation capabilities, Adrian completed CPA Canada’s In-depth Income Tax Program ↗ and earned full membership as a Trust and Estate Practitioner (TEP) with the prestigious Canadian branch of STEP (Society of Trust and Estate Practitioners) ↗.

Global Examination Distinction

Before immigrating to Canada, Adrian completed the grueling accounting credentials of both the Chartered Institute of Management Accountants (CIMA ↗) and the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA ↗), holding Fellow status (FCMA and FCCA) in both bodies. In the worldwide Stage 2 CIMA examinations of November 1996, Adrian achieved the second highest aggregate globally amongst 5,798 international candidates.

Work Ethic & Personal Life

Adrian is married and has three children. An early riser by discipline, he typically begins his workday around 4:00 AM, capitalizing on the early quiet hours to conduct rigorous tax planning and case law review before client meetings begin. Outside the office, he enjoys morning gym workouts, watching international cricket, and spending August on extended road trips exploring North America with his family.

Tania Joseph

Tax Associate & QuickBooks Specialist

Practice Associate

Delivering Rigorous Tax Compliance & Cloud Bookkeeping

Tania has been working with the firm for over five years. As a certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor, she spearheads the firm’s cloud bookkeeping implementations and coordinates compilation engagements in CaseWare.

Under Adrian's supervision, Tania prepares Canadian corporate and personal income tax filings, manages year-end financial reconciliations across QuickBooks Online and Desktop, and works directly with clients across diverse industry sectors including professional services, retail, and manufacturing.

Tania holds a Bachelor of Commerce (Honours) in Accounting with a minor in Entrepreneurship from Ontario Tech University ↗. During her academic career, she actively contributed to the university’s Accounting Association, Business & IT Society, and Women in Business Club. Complementing her work at the firm, Tania completed an intensive Business Tax internship at a Big Four accounting firm, providing her with elite exposure to multi-entity tax structures and technical statutory research.

Looking forward, Tania is actively pursuing her Chartered Professional Accountant (CPA) designation and Trust and Estate Practitioner (TEP) certification, with a dedicated specialization in corporate and personal tax planning.
